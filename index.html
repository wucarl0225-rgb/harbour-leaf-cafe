Our Story

Where Melbourne Meets Modern Asian Cuisine

Harbour Leaf Café blends Melbourne's vibrant café culture with bold Asian brunch flavours. Every dish is crafted with fresh, locally sourced ingredients and a passion for memorable dining.

A Unique Brunch Experience

Founded in the heart of Melbourne, Harbour Leaf Café has become the go-to destination for those seeking something beyond the ordinary brunch. Our chefs draw inspiration from across Asia — Japan, Korea, and Southeast Asia — to create dishes that are both exciting and comforting.

Alongside our signature dishes, we offer specialty coffee curated by expert baristas and a range of house-made drinks you won't find anywhere else.

  • Specialty coffee by local roasters
  • Asian-inspired modern brunch menu
  • Heritage building in central Melbourne
